Three looks for the car screen

Each panel is the car display at roughly 800×480, playing fake data that simulates a pull through the gears, then cruising, then idling. Which one feels closest, and what would you change?

A · Racing game

Gran Turismo / Forza style: big tachometer in the middle, shift lights across the top, temperatures on the left.

B · Fighter-jet HUD

Green vector lines and scrolling tapes, with temperatures as boxed readouts. Readable at a glance and very low-glare at night.

C · Retro arcade

Synthwave neon with a pixel font. RPM is a power bar, temperatures are stat bars, and fuel economy is your "score".
